About

A quiet gallery for considered work.

Duke & Lume Collection is a curated gallery of AI-generated art for the modern collector. The intent is simple: fewer works, chosen well, presented with the care a piece deserves.

Each work is conceived as an editorial object. Light, mood, texture, and restraint matter as much as the subject. The aim is presence — work that holds a room, not work that fills it.

License

Works in the collection are offered with clear licensing for personal collection, private display, and (where specified) limited commercial use. Each piece carries its own terms; details are confirmed at the point of inquiry.

Terms

All visual content remains the intellectual property of Duke & Lume Collection unless an explicit transfer is agreed in writing. Resale, reproduction, and derivative use are governed by the per-work license.

FAQ

Are these original works?

Yes — each piece is created and curated for the collection, not pulled from open libraries.

How do I acquire a piece?

Reach out through the Inquire page with the work title. Pricing, format, and licensing are confirmed by reply.

Do you accept commissions?

Selectively. Mention the brief in your inquiry and we'll respond if it fits the collection's direction.
